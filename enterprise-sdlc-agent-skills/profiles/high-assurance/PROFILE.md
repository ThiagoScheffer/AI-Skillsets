# High-Assurance Profile

Use where failure has severe security, safety, financial, public-service or operational consequences.

## Additions

- explicit threat model review
- independent or second-pass review for critical controls
- negative/abuse-case verification
- rollback and recovery rehearsal where feasible
- stronger change control
- dependency/supply-chain review
- privileged access review
- resilience/failure-injection strategy where justified
- explicit evidence retention
- human approval gates

Favor small reversible releases and measurable rollback conditions.
