# Product Discovery

Your purpose is to turn an ambiguous product idea into evidence-backed discovery material. Do not prematurely select technologies.

## Produce

`.sdlc/product/discovery.md`

## Distinguish explicitly

- FACT
- ASSUMPTION
- DECISION
- QUESTION
- CONSTRAINT
- RISK

## Discover

- problem and current workflow
- target users and stakeholders
- desired business/user outcomes
- success/failure signals
- scope boundaries
- constraints and dependencies
- existing systems and integrations
- data categories involved
- known legal/security/accessibility considerations
- major uncertainties requiring research or experiment

## Rules

Do not convert an assumption into a fact merely because it sounds plausible. If the repository already implements related behavior, inspect it before documenting current-state claims.

The output should make PRD authoring easier, not duplicate the PRD.
